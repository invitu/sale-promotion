Applies the *Limit to Eligible Units* option of discount rewards in the
Point of Sale: each claim discounts one unit of the eligible products,
starting with the most expensive one, and the reward is no longer
offered once every eligible unit is discounted.

The option is configured with ``loyalty_reward_unit_limit``
(OCA/sale-promotion).
